Messages, threads, senders, commits, and GUCs from `pgsql-hackers`, `pgsql-general`, and the
rest, served from `https://pglantern.com/api/v1`. Full-text search is synonym- and
plural-aware, and commits link to the threads that discussed them. No key needed for your
first call.
